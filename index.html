Our Legacy

Four Decades,
One Promise

Sisi Travel Egypt was founded in 1982 with a simple yet ambitious goal — to redefine travel in Egypt by providing world-class services with a personal touch. Over the past four decades, we've proudly served thousands of individual travelers, families, corporate clients, religious groups, and international partners.

We are more than a travel agency — we are a team of passionate professionals who understand that every journey is a personal story. From the first call to the final farewell, we provide seamless coordination, creative itineraries, and unmatched hospitality.
